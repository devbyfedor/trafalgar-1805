@@@ The army: figures for their own dates (a-v-merged-h2 11)
<<<
Napoleon's own round figure was 150,000 men and 10,000 horses (Corr. XI No. 9209). High.
===
Napoleon's own round figure was 150,000 men and 10,000 horses (Corr. XI No. 9209). Other figures are right for their own dates: the craft at Étaples, Boulogne, Wimereux and Ambleteuse could carry 120,000 men and 10,000 horses by Napoleon's count of 2 July 1804 (Corr. IX No. 7832), and the table of July 1805 was to carry 163,645 men, 16,783 of them seamen, and 9,059 horses (James III, App. 27, from Dumas; `research/scene-02/h2-boulogne-cadiz.md`, H2-03). High.
>>>

@@@ The decision, 22–26 August: Bernadotte, then James's 22nd (a-v-merged-h2 11 and 10)

@@@ Why east: Napoleon's figures for the army (a-v-merged-h2 11)
<<<
On the 25th he wrote that Austria's preparations had suspended his embarkation (No. 9130).
===
On the 25th he wrote that Austria's preparations had suspended his embarkation (No. 9130). His figures for the army he would bring varied with the reader: 200,000 in Germany (to Talleyrand, 23 and 25 August), 300,000 (for Marmont, 23 August) and "plus de 100,000" in Bavaria (to the King of Prussia, 23 August); none is a return (`research/scene-02/a-h2a.md`, R20).
>>>
