# The smoke at Finisterre (session 33, part one)

Frames of the production build of the branch `s33-smoke` at `b8c77ea` (served on port 4173 and checked with `scripts/dev/served.mjs`), taken with `scripts/dev/road.mjs`: 1280×720 CSS px at device scale 1.5, and one at 1920×1080. The camera holds over the action off Cape Finisterre from p 0.527 to 0.548; the story's clock runs through 22 July (`docs/decisions.md`, "Scene 2, Stage B: the smoke at Finisterre").

1. `01-before-the-guns-p0.533-1280.png`: about 3.40 p.m. The lines stand closing; no smoke.
2. `02-first-guns-p0.5385-1280.png`: about 5.40 p.m. The vans have opened fire (at 5.15, p 0.5372); the first puffs at the left, the van's end, and the fire running down the lines.
3. `03-growing-p0.542-1280.png`: about 7 p.m. Every ship firing; the smoke at the ships' sides toward the enemy and lying on the water between the lines, drifting right (astern) on the WNW breeze.
4. `04-thick-p0.546-1280.png`: about 9 p.m. The smoke at its thickest, filling the lane; the ships stand over it.
5. `05-pause-ends-p0.548-1280.png`: about 10 p.m., the end of the camera's pause. The firing stopped at 9.30; no new puffs. The label shows "20 sail" crossing to "18 sail" as the two prizes leave the line (as on `main`).
6. `06-thinning-p0.552-1280.png`: after midnight, the camera rising. The smoke thins (its lines go first, then its body) and drifts on.
7. `07-drifting-off-p0.556-1280.png`: the morning of the 23rd. The last of it, soft and faint, carried off astern; gone by about p 0.5607.
8. `08-thick-p0.546-1920.png`: frame 4 at 1920×1080.

What the frames do not show: the motion. Each puff swells, leans and drifts as the scroll goes on, and goes back the same way when scrolling back; at rest nothing moves.
