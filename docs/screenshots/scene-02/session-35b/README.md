# The day relay of 19 October, its signals (session 35, part two)

Frames of the production build of the branch `s35-relay` at `77d49eb` (build `o3ItMJ202FoLAIaNSGB0u`, served on port 4173 and checked with `scripts/dev/served.mjs`), taken with `scripts/dev/road.mjs`: 1280×720 CSS px at device scale 1.5 (the owner's screen) unless named 2560 (2560×1440 at scale 1). The signal No. 370, "the enemy's ships are coming out of port", runs down the chain from the Sirius off San Sebastián to the Victory: three flags at each link's main truck (the same three at every link), counted gun puffs at her side, the Phoebe's three-minute guns, the Mars's colours and then the distant signal, the Victory's one flag and two guns while the camera holds over Nelson's station. See `docs/decisions.md`, "Scene 2, Stage B: the relay of 19 October, part two".

1. `01-sirius-hoists-p0.766-1280.png`: 7.00 a.m. The camera has come out of the bay; the Sirius hoists No. 370 and fires (the frigates off the port are small here: six craft within a mile or two of each other).
2. `02-euryalus-and-phoebe-p0.772-1280.png`: about 7.25. The Euryalus has repeated it and sent the Phoebe west; the Phoebe hoists it and fires her first guns.
