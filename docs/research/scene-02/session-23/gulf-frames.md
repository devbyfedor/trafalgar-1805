# The Gulf frames: where a fleet in the frame shows neither its label nor its ships (session 23)

The owner's rule (12 November 2026): a fleet in the frame shows as a readable label or as its ships, never neither; a fleet off the frame is recorded and left. Session 22 flagged rows at p 0.68, 0.685–0.695, 0.78 and 0.82 without knowing whether the fleet was in the frame.

**How measured.** `scripts/dev/fleet-frame.mjs` (new): for each side of the October fleets, the ships of the line with the fleet then, how many stand in the frame (their places projected with the frame's own camera, drawn or not), how many are drawn at half opacity or more, the gate, how far the ships' middle lies into the haze (its distance over the haze's visibility) and how steeply the view meets it, the label's opacity and its best place's room and what limits it. Production build of `9fd8b2f` (served build checked), 1280×720@1.5, 13 November 2026. Step 1 does not change these frames (`look-diff.mjs`: identical from p 0.24 on). Frames looked at: 0.68, 0.69, 0.70, 0.71, 0.775, 0.79, 0.815, 0.82.

**What is wrong, stretch by stretch.**
- **p 0.6825–0.705, coming down to the harbour (daylight).** The combined fleet (33 of the line at anchor in the bay) is in the frame. Its label fades from 0.60 at 0.675 to nothing by 0.6825 and is back only at 0.705 (0.84); its ships come in only in the harbour's hold (drawn about 0.728–0.742; the label stands for them 0.705–0.725 and 0.745–0.765). Two causes: every place within 3.7 em of the anchorage lies on the waterlines or across the coast (ground 0), and the ships' middle lies 0.48–0.55 of the haze's visibility away, where the label is cut (0.42–0.55). Breaks the rule.
- **p 0.77–0.825, climbing out and over the Gulf (day turning to dusk).** The same for the combined fleet coming out on the 19th and 20th: 33 in the frame, none drawn, label 0 (ground 0; haze 0.49–0.55), a flicker of 0.02–0.04 at 0.78 and 0.82. Near the harbour only the inshore frigates show (not ships of the line; drawn because they read one by one). Breaks the rule.
- **p 0.6775–0.695, the British label.** 0.72, then 0.40 at 0.685, 0.68 at 0.69, 0.33 at 0.695, while all 27 stand in the frame (the haze cut: their middle at 0.47–0.54); its figure changes 27, 28, 27 there. From 0.6975 the fleet leaves the frame (4 ships in it at 0.695, none at 0.7). Weak while in the frame: breaks the rule.
- **p 0.775, the British fleet.** 4 of 27 in the frame, its label's every place off the frame: to look at with the fix.
- **The crossfades at p 0.06 (the rise at dawn) and 0.97–0.98 (the last descent).** Label and ships together come to about half (at 0.9725 the combined label 0.17, its ships 0.34): the label is dimmed by the view's tilt and by the hand-over to the ships at once (`HeadLabels`: presence × (1 − handover) × tilt × haze).
- **Off the frame, recorded and left:** the British fleet from 0.6975 to 0.775 and 0.805–0.815 (none of its ships in the frame).

**Why the haze cut is where it is.** The haze turns the ground toward the colour of the air, `1 − exp(−x²)` at x of the visibility (`terrain/shaders.ts`, `hazeAmount`; colour: the sky at the horizon, toward the air's own colour as the view looks down, `shaders/common.ts`). Worked from the light states' colours (`light/states.ts`): the pale ink (lifted ×1.12) against the brightest daylight sea passes under 4.7:1 from about x 0.35–0.47, so the measured cut at 0.55 is what the daylight views allow; at night the hazed sea stays dark and the pale ink keeps about 5.3:1 even at full haze; the dark ink on the daylight paper keeps 7:1 or more under any haze, but on the night paper only about 2.2:1 (no label should stand on the land at night).

**The fix being built** (branch `wip/s23-gulf-labels`, its commit message has the whole plan): two farther places about a fleet's head (5.2 and 6.7 em), tried last; the haze judged at each place, not at the fleet, from the frame's own light (never stricter than now on the sea by day; at dusk and night the pale ink reads on; on the land by day the dark ink reads on, at night no land place); the label's tilt and hand-over taken as the lesser of the two, not their product, so the crossfades don't dip. To check after: `fleet-frame.mjs` over 0.67–0.83 and at 0.06 and 0.97–0.98 (no row flagged), `contrast.mjs` over 0.65–0.83 and the night, `look-diff.mjs` (labels change only where they were weak), the frames looked at.
