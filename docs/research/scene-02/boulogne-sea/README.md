# Boulogne from the sea: research notes

Research for scene 2, beat 1 ("Boulogne from the sea"): the painting of Boulogne's roads, flotilla, town and camps on the evening of 10 August 1805, seen from a boat in the roads looking east. All written on 13 November 2026 (sessions 24–26) under the rules in `../../README.md`: our own words with links, and short quotations from public-domain period sources only where the wording is the evidence. Confidence uses the scale at the top of `docs/history-notes.md`.

The project's summary of these files is `docs/history-notes.md`, "Scene 2: Boulogne from the sea (beat 1)". The liberties the painting takes are decisions, in `docs/decisions.md`, "Boulogne from the sea". Where a check corrects its note, the check wins.

## The files

- `evening.md`: the chosen evening, 10 August 1805, computed for the painting's final eye: the sun and the painting's light (18:33), the moon against the skyline at sunset, the tide and the strand, the weather; the liberties ready for `decisions.md` and the numbers for the painter. Complete (session 26). Not yet checked by an independent reader; it builds on the checked figures of `light-and-watch-check.md`.
- `light-and-watch.md`: the sun, moon, weather, sea and tide of August 1805 evenings, the Royal Navy's watch, and whether England shows. Complete (session 24). Its tide table is refuted by its check for the times of low water: use the check and `evening.md`.
- `light-and-watch-check.md`: its independent check, with the sun and moon recomputed (JPL Horizons) and the tide corrected (TICON-3 constants). Complete (session 26).
- `flotilla.md`: the flotilla's craft (types, sizes, rigs, guns, colours, flags) and how they lay in the harbour and in the roads. Complete (session 24).
- `flotilla-check.md`: its check. Complete (session 28): the list "What the painter must change or know" and the verdicts 2.1–2.5 were written in session 25; the verdicts 2.6–2.13 (other craft, colours, flags, sizes, how they lay, at anchor, motion, images), the verdict on section 1, the recomputations, the additional facts and the sources opened were added in session 28, with items 10–13 of the list.
- `camps.md`: the hut camps on the heights and what the roads saw of them, Napoleon's pavilion and its neighbours, tents, and the other things on the heights. Complete (session 24; the file has no status line).
- `camps-check.md`: its check. Complete (session 26).
- `town-and-defences.md`: the town from the roads (upper town, belfry and telegraph, churches, lower town, harbour), what did not stand in August 1805, the shore defences (the stone forts, the wooden fort, the pierhead battery, the batteries, the signal posts) and the period views. Complete (session 24).
- `town-and-defences-check.md`: its check. Complete (session 26).
