Hoists stay up until the Victory answers, so lit links accumulate westward, then come down as the answer runs back. Neither is logged on the 19th: the held hoists follow the rules, and the counted puffs follow the counted guns the Euryalus logged with her signals on the 20th (NRS II 165, 167), while along the chain the records of the 19th have only guns fired by the Phoebe and by a ship seen from the fleet (NRS II 146, 312, 322; see "Assumptions and licences"). @@@ Later inventions: firework colour (a-v-merged-h3 9)
<<<
Vivid red or green fireworks are wrong too: before about 1811 firework colours were weak reds and yellows at most (Physics World; RSC). Medium.
===
Vivid red or green fireworks are wrong too: before about 1811 firework colour was weak at most, pale reds and yellows by *Physics World*, faint greens and reds by the RSC (Lancaster 2012). Werrett (2015) argues that early modern manuals already gave a variety of recipes for coloured fire and that the later stress on colour comes from writers of the Chemical Revolution; the French remarks on the colour of the British fires are read under "Night lights". Medium.
>>>
