4. `04-branch-relay-lowest-p0.7563.png`: the branch, the same frame: the label on the open sea past the tip (0.88).
5. `05-branch-dip-scrolling-back-p0.7088.png`: the branch, scrolling back at 300 px/s: a dip that remains (the label at 0.40 here, 0.20 at its lowest, 0.25 s). The frigates of the inshore watch come in as they grow readable and sweep across the place the label had just taken.
