# The lanterns at night (session 33, part two)

Frames of the production build of the branch `s33-smoke` at `4b86366` (served on port 4173 and checked with `scripts/dev/served.mjs`), taken with `scripts/dev/road.mjs`: 1280×720 CSS px at device scale 1.5 (the owner's screen), and one at 1920×1080 at scale 1. The fleets come back into the story as the camera returns from Ulm (p 0.905 to 0.925), and the camera comes down over them to the last frame at dawn; the story's clock runs from about 10.30 p.m. on 20 October to 6.36 a.m. on the 21st (`docs/decisions.md`, "Scene 2, Stage B: the lanterns at night").

1. `01-fleets-return-p0.92-1280.png`: about 11 p.m., the camera high over the Gulf. A few warm points where each fleet is, beside its label; the line still too small to show more (its lights give way to each other so they never run together).
2. `02-descent-p0.935-1280.png`: after midnight, coming down. The combined fleet's two lines begin to show as strings of lights.
3. `03-the-lit-line-p0.95-1280.png`: about 2 a.m. The combined fleet's line lit "like a well lit up street": a light at every ship's stern, the flagships' top lights, Gravina's at his mizzen (leading the western line). The British dark but for two pairs of lights, the Victory's and the Royal Sovereign's (stern and top), each with its reflection on the sea under it.
4. `04-four-am-p0.96-1280.png`: about 4 a.m., lower still; the lines and the light coming up over the coast.
5. `05-before-dawn-p0.965-1280.png`: about 5 a.m. The Royal Sovereign's lights have faded at the caption's card (lights fade as they come near it, as under its scrim); the Victory's below.
6. `06-daylight-going-out-p0.97-1280.png`: about 6 a.m., the logs' "daylight". The lights going out; the ships begin to show.
7. `07-out-p0.9735-1280.png`: about 6.20 a.m. All out; the ships of the line come in as the day comes.
8. `08-the-lit-line-p0.95-1920.png`: frame 3 at 1920×1080 (scale 1: the lights are as many CSS pixels, so fewer device pixels than on the owner's screen).
9. `09-zoom-the-lit-line-p0.95.png`: frame 3 enlarged three times about the combined fleet (nearest-neighbour).
10. `10-zoom-the-victory-p0.965.png`: frame 5 enlarged six times about the Victory: her stern light, her top light, and the broken column of each one's reflection.

What the frames do not show: the motion. The lights keep to their ships as the camera turns 157 degrees and comes down; a line thins out evenly as it recedes and fills in as it nears; a light passing under a label goes out over a quarter of a second and comes back the same way. Nothing flickers or breathes: a lantern burns steadily from when it is lit to when it is put out.
