# Boulogne from the sea: stage B, part one, from the golden light to dusk

Frames of the production build of `9f3508e`, served locally and checked with `scripts/dev/served.mjs`, shot with `scripts/dev/sea-shots.mjs` at the owner's size (1280×720 CSS px at device scale 1.5), the quality level pinned to the top. Session 29, 14 November 2026. The points are the interlude's own parts (`window.__road.scrollForInterlude`): the dive is 1.2 screens of scroll, the hold now 3, the rise 1.2. Through the hold the evening goes from the golden light of 18:33 through the sun's last light to the dusk of about 19:45 (`src/scenes/02-road/boulogne-sea/paint/dusk.ts`; decisions.md, session 29).

1. `01-hold-0.00-golden.png`: the arrival, the approved golden painting (the line's craft now every 80 m, the chaloupe's ensign on its staff at the taffrail).
2. `02-hold-0.20-golden-ends.png`: the golden light at its end; the camera has begun its drift.
3. `03-hold-0.32-last-light.png`: the sun's last light: the land's lit faces orange-red, the sky darkening over it.
4. `04-hold-0.45-sunset.png`: the sunset: the land loses the sun from the water up, the clouds blaze salmon and rose.
5. `05-hold-0.55-land-in-shade.png`: the land in shade, the clouds still lit; the Earth's shadow rising under the Belt of Venus.
6. `06-hold-0.65-dusk-coming.png`: the dusk coming: the fires behind the camps' crests, the first lights, the moon brightening.
7. `07-hold-0.80-lights.png`: the lights coming up in the camps and the town; the moon's path on the water.
8. `08-hold-1.00-dusk.png`: the dusk at the hold's end: the moon whole, clear of the chaloupe's rigging; the far water and the wet strand mirroring the sky.
9. `09-hold-1.00-right-camp-and-pavilion-2x.png`: the right camp and Napoleon's pavilion at dusk, twice the size: doors and casements lit in clusters, the kitchens' fires glowing behind the crest under their smoke, the pavilion's lamps.
10. `10-hold-1.00-town-2x.png`: the lower town's few lit windows at civil dusk.
11. `11-hold-1.00-moon-and-its-path-2x.png`: the moon and its path on the water.
12. `12-rise-0.36-dusk-to-engraving.png`: on the rise the dusk turns back into the engraving, then the chart.
