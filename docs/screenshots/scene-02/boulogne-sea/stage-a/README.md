# Boulogne from the sea: stage A, the dive, the hold and the rise

Frames of the production build of `fd6c61b` (source as at `747f247`), served locally and checked with `scripts/dev/served.mjs`, shot with `scripts/dev/sea-shots.mjs` at the owner's size (1280×720 CSS px at device scale 1.5), the quality level pinned to the top. Session 28, 14 November 2026. The points are the interlude's own parts (`window.__road.scrollForInterlude`): the dive is 1.2 screens of scroll, the hold 2.4, the rise 1.2.

1. `01-dive-0.00-chart.png`: the chart's view of Boulogne, where the dive begins.
2. `02-dive-0.30-swoop.png`: the camera swooping down and round toward the roads; the text has stepped aside.
3. `03-dive-0.50-engraving.png`: the chart turned into an engraving of the same view, lines on the chart's paper.
4. `04-dive-0.56-colour-begins.png`: the first wash of colour over the engraving, the land's warmth leading.
5. `05-dive-0.65-colour-coming.png`: the colour coming in while the camera is still coming down.
6. `06-hold-0.00-arrival.png`: the view from the sea, the text back over the painting.
7. `07-hold-0.50.png`: the middle of the hold; the camera drifts along the anchored line.
8. `08-hold-1.00-end.png`: the hold's end: the moon clear between the near chaloupe's mainmast and her ensign, her jib-boom's reflection short of the caption (the owner's small items, session 28).
9. `09-rise-0.45-colour-leaving.png`: on the rise the colour goes first.
10. `10-rise-0.50-engraving.png`: the engraving again.
11. `11-rise-1.00-chart.png`: back on the chart, where the scene goes on as before.
