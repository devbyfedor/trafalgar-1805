# Research notes

The full research behind `docs/history-notes.md`, written during session 1 and fact-checked:

- `victory-hull.md`: Victory's hull as of October 1805.
- `victory-rig.md`: masts, spars, sails, rigging and flags.
- `dawn-setting.md`: positions, sun, moon, weather and both fleets, with a fact-check of 54 claims.

## What belongs here

The repository is public, so this folder holds only our own notes: findings in our own words, measurements and computations, tables, and links to the sources.

- **Modern sources** (museum and official-site pages, press releases, interviews, articles, books, Wikipedia, forums, kit manuals): paraphrased with a citation and a link, never copied. Names and short terms ("Victory Hull Ochre") are fine.
- **Period sources in the public domain** (the 1805 logs, Steel 1794, Lever 1808, the 1808 Regulations, Beatty 1807, Nicolas 1846, James, Corbett 1910, Fraser 1906, Desbrière 1907, Perrin 1922): short quotations of a phrase or a sentence are kept where the exact wording is the evidence, for example "out fourth reef of main topsail". Longer passages are paraphrased with a page reference.
- **No copies of sources:** no PDFs, page images, saved web pages or downloaded texts. If a later session needs to keep such files while it works, it puts them in `docs/research/sources/`, which git ignores, and they never leave the machine.
