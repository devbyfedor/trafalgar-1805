# Boulogne from the roads, August 1805: the town and the shore defences

Research note for scene 2, beat 1 ("Boulogne from the sea"): what of the town of Boulogne-sur-Mer and of the defences of its roads could be seen from a boat anchored in the roads on an August evening in 1805, at what size, colour and place, and how sure we are.

Work in progress. Sections still to be filled are marked *(to come)*.

Confidence follows the scale in `docs/history-notes.md`: **High**, several independent primary sources agree, or a computation confirmed a second way; **Medium**, one good source or a consistent inference; **Low**, an estimate, a measurement from a picture, or sources in conflict. Each fact also says what it rests on: **period** (documents, memoirs, plans and images of the time), **later scholarly** (Desbrière, James, Clowes, Troude and the like), **modern** (recent works and web pages, used as leads) or **inference** (ours).
